## Decisions made

- **CC redaction by hash**: SHA-256 prefix (16 chars) plus role and
  approximate token count, not the raw text. Even an attacker who
  obtains a NATS dump cannot reconstruct transcript content.
- **Polling over notify(2)** for the first cut. notify is a separate
  follow-up; polling is simpler to reason about, idempotent against
  cursor on restart, and the 5s interval is fine for a daily-cadence
  synthesis pipeline.
- **`PayloadSink` channel split** instead of a kind-discriminator on a
  single method. Routing happens in the trait, not in `match`
  branches inside the sink, so future ingest sources (GH webhooks,
  Slack) plug in by adding a new method rather than growing a string
  enum.
- **CostGuard wraps LlmClient** (newtype, not a flag on the API).
  Lets synthesis / triage / embedding each pick the policy variant
  they need and compose with retries / circuit breakers later.
